We'd like to welcome Manus as a Platinum Sponsor of the Godot Foundation. Their contribution helps keep Godot free, open, and in constant development for everyone, and we truly appreciate it. Here is a message from them:

### A message from Manus:

> AI is lowering the technical barriers to making games, but a tool is only ever a tool. A good game is one you and your friends love to play over and over, and that comes from your sense of how it should feel, the little details, and the odd ideas only you would have. From here on, what's rare isn't the ability to build, it's knowing what you want to build.  
>   
> Godot has done more than almost anyone to put real game-making tools in everyone's hands: an open-source engine shaped by its community and kept going by contributors and paid developers who care about it. We're proud to support that work, and we hope more people who make their first game with Manus will go on to explore what they can create with Godot.  
>   
> — Tao Zhang, Co-founder & CPO, Manus AI

Manus is the world's first general AI agent that turns ideas into finished work. To learn more, read the Game Dev blog post: [Introducing Game Dev: Enabling Everyone to Make the Games They Want to Play](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-game-developer).
