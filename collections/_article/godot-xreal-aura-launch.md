[XREAL AURA](https://www.xreal.com/aura), the first optical see-through glasses on Android XR, [is here](https://us.shop.xreal.com/products/xreal-aura?utm_source=social&utm_medium=extension&utm_campaign=AURA_Launch&utm_content=Godot_Engine) and Godot is part of the launch lineup\!

## Develop natively on spatial computing glasses

Download the [Godot editor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.godotengine.editor.v4) and its [companion app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.godotengine.godot_gradle_build_environment) on the XREAL AURA, and develop, export, and publish spatial Godot games and apps natively on XREAL AURA.  
The [Godot editor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.godotengine.editor.v4) on XREAL AURA provides the [features](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/list_of_features.html#editor) that you'd come to expect and love on desktop within a multi-window spatial workspace that goes with you.

Download Godot editor on Google Play: [https\://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.godotengine.editor.v4](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.godotengine.editor.v4) 

## Build spatial games and apps

Godot continues the trend of being one of the lightest, most flexible, and best engines for XR development by delivering a [plethora of new features](https://developer.android.com/develop/xr/godot) to provide developers access to the newest Android XR capabilities available on XREAL AURA.

This includes support for the new [OpenXR Spatial Containers](https://www.khronos.org/blog/openxr-spatial-containers), which are system-managed volumes in which applications can display spatial content, similar to how traditional applications draw 2D content within windows on a desktop.

On XREAL AURA, Godot spatial games and apps come alive right on your table, anchored exactly where you place it, with hand tracking and a low-latency spatial display that make every move feel natural.

These new spatial capabilities lower the barrier of entry for developers to build new spatial games and apps, or port existing 3D games.

### Porting DOGWALK to XREAL AURA

To showcase the new [OpenXR Spatial Containers](https://www.khronos.org/blog/openxr-spatial-containers) on XREAL AURA, and the ease through which developers can port their 3D games to Spatial Containers, the [Godot Foundation](https://godot.foundation/) in partnership with [Blender Studio](https://studio.blender.org/welcome/) and [W4 Games](https://www.w4games.com/), is working on a spatial port of [DOGWALK](https://studio.blender.org/projects/dogwalk/) to XREAL AURA.

This project builds on the ongoing [collaboration](https://godotengine.org/article/announcing-android-xr-support/) between the Godot Foundation and W4 Games to expand Godot's Android XR support. Working closely together, the teams have tackled the engineering challenges and optimizations involved in bringing the spatial version of [DOGWALK](https://studio.blender.org/projects/dogwalk/) to XREAL AURA in a relatively short time, and are now hard at work polishing the game for a release later this year. Stay tuned!
