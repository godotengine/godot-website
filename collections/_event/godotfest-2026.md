The second GodotFest happens on the **3rd and 4th of November** in Munich, Germany.

After close to 500 attendees and 34 speakers joined us for the first edition, GodotFest is
returning for two more days of talks, panels and games from across the Godot
community.

Come meet the people building with Godot, swap ideas with other developers, and enjoy premium
food and drinks throughout the event, all included in your ticket.
