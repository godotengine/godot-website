Join the Godot community in Shenzhen for a day of talks and discussion about community news, open-source projects, and creations made with Godot. Emilio, Executive Director of the Godot Foundation, will attend to meet local developers. Godot users of all experience levels, as well as anyone interested in games or open source, are welcome.

2026 年 11 月 28 日 9:00–18:00，Godot Meetup 将在深圳大学粤海校区艺术综合楼举行。我们将围绕 Godot 社群动态、开源项目与开发者作品展开分享与交流，欢迎不同经验水平的 Godot 用户、以及对游戏和开源感兴趣的朋友参加。Godot 基金会执行董事埃米利奥将来到现场，与大家交流。请从深南大道北门进校，购票与免票政策详见[活动页面](https://godothub.com/event/godot-meetup-shenzhen-2026)。
