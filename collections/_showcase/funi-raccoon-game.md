Funi Raccoon Game, a 3D Platformer Collectathon. You play as a raccoon who has recently acquired a new home (Dumpster).

Home is where the stuff is, but your home is pretty empty. Fill up your dumpster with cool, unique stuff. The more stuff you have, the more places you can go! Travel the lands, "help" the locals, and solve puzzles -- all in the aim to get more stuff!?!

### Features

- Pick Up and Throw Stuff
- Use that stuff for problem solving
- The more stuff you have, the more new areas you unlock
- Your stuff enables a multitude of traversal possibilities for those willing to seize the keys of opportunity
- Travel to various microworlds all interconnected in whimsical ways
