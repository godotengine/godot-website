### KEY FEATURES

- Your Reign, Your Rules - Every choice you make defines your legacy and the fate of your Kingdom...

- Recruit eccentric Knights - Manage a court of unpredictable heroes with big egos and even bigger problems.

- Manage Your Round Table - Send Knights on quests where success or disaster hinges on your judgment.

- Bend Time and Tempt Fate - Rewind decisions, uncover secrets, and pursue forbidden romances.

- Expand Your Tower - Build new annexes, acquire upgrades, and outwit rival rulers.

### THE THRONE IS YOURS

Ah, finally - a suitable Sovereign for the job. Yes, you, right there, come sit on this comfy throne. Ah you like the feel of that, don’t you? Congratulations, you’re hired! My name is Arlin and I’m your advisor. Welcome to your first day, and please, allow me to provide thee with a day in the life of a Sovereign and you can then decide how will you rule, my liege...

### YOUR AUDIENCE AWAITS

- In the morning you must tend to your subjects and listen to the problems that plague the Kingdom. From gardening to exorcisms - there’s never a boring day on the throne.

- Each decision you make will shape your kingdom and relationships with individuals, collectives and other rulers. Every interaction will play a part in defining your legacy.

- Balance your affinity with the Merchants, Mystics, Scholars, Nobles and People, as well as the Treasury, or spend it all and backstab everyone. Either way works, my grace.

### KNIGHTS IN SHINING DRAMA

- Recruit eccentric Knights with strong personalities and questionable morals to serve at your Round Table. Unfortunately for you, these are the jesters you’re relying on to keep your Kingdom running smoothly.

- With great egos, comes great drama! These Knights aren’t going to be a walk in the Tower’s Garden, sire. There’s always advice to be taken, grievances to be shared and romance to be explored.

- You must keep your Knights satisfied otherwise they may just leave your court for good. Maybe that’s sometimes for the best though...

### ROUND TABLE MANAGEMENT

- Manage your Knights from the Round Table. Here you can view all the requests from your subjects that you obliged to earlier in the day. Assign the most equipped Knight for the job to reduce chances of failure.

- Your Knights possess stats: Strength, Agility, Charisma, Magic and Wit. Each Quest requires a different skill set. Need a Charismatic approach? Maybe we shouldn’t send Ursula, your grace.

- After they return from their Quests, you’ll be debriefed with the results of their venture. Did their efforts result in Critical Failure or Critical Success? Or did they experience a completely Unexpected Outcome?

### REWINDING TIME, RUMOURS & ROMANCING

- In the Crypts of the Tower dwells a Demon who can turn back time. So, if it all goes wrong, you can go back with all the knowledge you’ve learned, make alternate decisions and change fate.

- Of course, you have me, your main advisor at your service, but there are others. For example, The Intendant has a nosy habit of collecting secrets about your Knights. She’ll provide the juicy gossip as you require it it!

- If you like romancing stone statues, then you’ve come to the right place. No, really, go speak to the Lady of the Tower and find out.

### EXPAND YOUR TOWER AND YOUR REIGN

- Prove your worthiness against opposers by joining hands with neighbouring counties and forging great alliances.

- Expand your Tower by adding new Annexes which in turn will allow for more positions to open amongst your court.

- From Carina’s Forge to the Witch’s Alchemy Room, each of these new Annexes gives you the ability to craft new items to assist your Knights on their Quests.
