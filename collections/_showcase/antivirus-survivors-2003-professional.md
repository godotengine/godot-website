### Double-Click

In Antivirus Survivors 2003 Professional, double-click through folders as you dodge voids, fight viruses, evade spam pop-ups, and defeat bosses. Search for XP and keys by exploring the folders and opening files, completing tasks and policies for bonuses. Develop your build through upgrades and plugins, and unlock new weapons and styles of gameplay.

With intense rogue-like action and a deep love for early 2000s computer operating systems, this is a blast of pure Y2K nostalgia served in roughly 30-40 minute runs.
